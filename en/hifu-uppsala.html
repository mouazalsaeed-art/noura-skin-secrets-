What is HIFU?

HIFU stands for High Intensity Focused Ultrasound. The ultrasound heats the deeper layers of the skin at selected points while the surface is left untouched. The heat stimulates the skin's own collagen production.

Because collagen builds up gradually, the result appears over the weeks after the treatment – for a natural lift and firmer skin. You can go straight back to your day.

Suits you if you have

  • Looser skin and less defined facial contours
  • Double chin and an undefined jawline
  • Fine lines around the eyes
  • A décolletage that has lost firmness
  • Loose skin on the stomach, thighs or upper arms

Step by step

How the treatment works

  1. ConsultationWe go through the areas you want treated and the result you want.
  2. CleansingThe area is cleansed and an ultrasound gel is applied.
  3. HIFU treatmentThe ultrasound is passed over the skin line by line – it feels like warmth and small pricks.
  4. Straight backNo downtime. The result builds over the coming weeks.
Good to know

Questions about HIFU

Does HIFU hurt?

It feels like warmth and short pricks at certain points, mostly over bone. We adjust the intensity so it is comfortable for you.

When will I see results?

Some see fresher skin straight away, but the real lift comes gradually as new collagen forms in the weeks after the treatment.

Do I need time off afterwards?

No. The skin may be slightly red or tender for a short while, but you can usually go straight back to work and daily life.

Who should not have HIFU?

During pregnancy, with implants or fillers in the area, active skin problems or certain illnesses – contact us before booking and we will assess whether the treatment suits you.
