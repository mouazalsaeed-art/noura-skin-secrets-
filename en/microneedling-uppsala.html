What is microneedling?

In microneedling, a tool with very fine needles creates small, controlled micro-channels in the skin. This starts the skin's own renewal and collagen production, and lets the active serums work deeper than in a regular facial.

You choose the solution for your skin's needs – for example renewal with DNA Salmon, pigmentation with tranexamic acid, hydration with hyaluronic acid or scar treatment. We look at your skin and suggest what suits you best.

Suits you if you have

  • Dull or tired skin that needs glow
  • Enlarged pores and uneven texture
  • Pigmentation and uneven skin tone
  • Fine lines and looser skin
  • Acne scars and other superficial scars

Good to know

Questions about Microneedling

Does microneedling hurt?

Most people feel a light pricking or vibrating sensation. We use numbing cream when needed so the treatment feels comfortable.

What does the skin look like afterwards?

It is common for the skin to be pink or slightly red and feel tight for a short while. Avoid make-up, sun and exercise right after – you get clear aftercare advice from us.

How many treatments do I need?

It depends on your goal. Many notice a difference after the first session, while scars and pigmentation are often treated in a series a few weeks apart. We make a plan together.

Is microneedling suitable for everyone?

During pregnancy, breastfeeding, active acne, skin infection or if you take certain medicines – contact us before booking and we will go through what is suitable for you.
